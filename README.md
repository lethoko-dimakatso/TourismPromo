# Discover Lesotho - Destination Travel Blog and Digital Storytelling Website

## Project Overview
A professional tourism website promoting Lesotho as a travel destination, featuring digital storytelling, travel blogs, and comprehensive tourism information.

## Assignment Requirements Met
- **5 Complete Pages**: Home, Travel Stories, Top Attractions, Travel Tips, Gallery & Social Media
- **HTML & CSS Only**: Pure HTML5 and CSS3 implementation
- **CSS Box Model Applied**: Extensive use of padding, margins, borders throughout
- **Responsive Design**: Mobile-first approach with breakpoints for tablets and phones
- **Tourism Content**: Rich content about Lesotho's attractions, culture, and travel experiences
- **Internal Navigation**: Fully linked navigation menu across all pages
- **Visual Appeal**: Professional design with consistent styling and branding

## Website Structure

### 1. Home Page (index.html)
- Hero banner with stunning landscape imagery
- Introduction to Lesotho tourism
- Quick highlights of key attractions
- Call-to-action buttons
- Responsive navigation menu

### 2. Travel Stories (travel-stories.html)
- 4+ engaging blog posts about Lesotho experiences
- Digital storytelling with personal narratives
- Cultural immersion stories
- Adventure travel experiences
- Tags and categorization

### 3. Top Attractions (attractions.html)
- Natural attractions (Thaba Bosiu, Maletsunyane Falls, etc.)
- Cultural heritage sites (Museums, monuments, villages)
- Adventure tourism activities (Pony trekking, rock art tours)
- Interactive filtering system
- Detailed descriptions and practical information

### 4. Travel Tips (travel-tips.html)
- Accommodation options (luxury, mid-range, budget)
- Transportation information
- Safety and health guidelines
- Best time to visit by season
- Local culture and etiquette
- What to pack recommendations
- Tables for organized information display

### 5. Gallery & Social Media (gallery.html)
- Image gallery with filtering (landscapes, culture, adventure, wildlife)
- Social media integration (Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, YouTube)
- Contact information and forms
- Recent social media posts feed
- Interactive contact form
